## ClinVar

NCBI places no restriction of its own on reuse of its molecular databases, and says some submitters may still claim rights.

Source: `https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/`, section "Molecular Data Usage", opened 10 October 2026. The page names molecular variation among those databases. NCBI itself places no restrictions on the use or distribution of the data contained therein, and it does not accept data when the submitter requested restrictions on reuse. Some submitters, or the country of origin, may claim patent, copyright, or other rights. NCBI cannot grant those rights. The same page asks that NLM be acknowledged for public-domain NLM web pages.

The words read are the `ClinicalSignificance` and `LastEvaluated` columns of `variant_summary`, as described in `https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/clinvar/README.txt`.
